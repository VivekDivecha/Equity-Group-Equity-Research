# Equity Group Holdings Plc — Equity Research \& Valuation



##### Overview



An independent equity research project analysing Equity Group Holdings Plc (NSE: EQTY) over FY2022–FY2025. The project evaluates the Group's financial performance, operating trends, asset quality and valuation relative to selected listed banking peers.



The analysis culminates in a valuation-based investment recommendation supported by financial statement analysis, peer benchmarking and scenario analysis.



##### Research Scope



* Financial performance: Revenue and profitability trends, return on equity (ROE), return on assets (ROA) and earnings growth.
* Operating efficiency and asset quality: Cost-to-income ratio (CIR), gross non-performing loan (NPL) ratio and changes in credit provisions.
* Peer benchmarking: Comparison with KCB Group, Co-operative Bank of Kenya and NCBA Group using profitability, asset quality and valuation metrics.
* Valuation: CAPM-based cost of equity and a single-stage Gordon Growth justified price-to-book (P/B) framework, supplemented by peer valuation comparisons and scenario analysis.
* Investment conclusion: Assessment of the relationship between profitability, valuation and the key assumptions underpinning the investment case.



##### Valuation Conclusion



The analysis arrives at a BUY recommendation with a price target of KES 101.97 per share, based on the justified P/B valuation framework.



The investment thesis considers the Group's profitability relative to its banking peers, its valuation relative to book value and the sustainability of returns. The conclusion remains subject to the assumptions and limitations described below.

##### Key Assumptions and Limitations



This is an independent student research and portfolio project, not institutional investment research. The valuation uses a single-stage justified P/B framework rather than a multi-year forecast or discounted cash flow model. The cost of equity and long-term growth assumptions are model inputs, and beta is assumed to be 1.0.



The peer comparison is limited to three listed Kenyan banking groups. Not all peer inputs, historical market prices and benchmark index figures have been independently re-verified. Differences between model-calculated and reported financial ratios may also arise from differences in definitions and calculation bases.



The valuation is therefore sensitive to its assumptions and should be interpreted as an analytical estimate rather than a precise prediction of future share price performance.



##### Data Sources



The analysis draws on Equity Group's annual reports, integrated reports and audited financial statements, alongside publicly available peer financial information and market data. Additional secondary sources are referenced in the research report where applicable.
